wger Workout Manager project (https://wger.de and https://github.com/wger-project).
They are redistributed under the license attached to each source record:
CC BY-SA 3.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, or CC0. The original authors include Everkinetic,
Lynn McIntyre, Settebello, Behrooz, and the respective wger contributors.
